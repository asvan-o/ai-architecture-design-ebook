[6] 5대 건축 프롬프트 키워드 조합 사전 (한글 · 영문 병기)
====================================================================
1) 카메라 구도 (Camera View & Framing)
- 탑뷰 / 정사영 평면: Orthographic Top-Down Plan, 90-degree flat 2D architectural plan
- 버드뷰 / 부감도: Bird's-Eye View, 45-degree high-angle architectural view
- 조감도 / 드론 뷰: Aerial Perspective, Drone View, 30-degree elevated site overview
- 아이소메트릭 / 액소노메트릭: Isometric 3D Cutaway, Axonometric Architectural Diagram
- 눈높이 투시도: Eye-Level View, Two-Point Perspective, vertical lines straight

2) 도면 클린업 (Cleanup & Simplification)
- 가구·집기 제거: Remove all furniture, fixtures, and clutter
- 문자·치수·메모 제거: Remove all text labels, dimension lines, grid bubbles, and red markups
- 구조선만 유지: Keep only structural walls, columns, windows, and doors with swing arcs
- 2D 선화 통일: Clean black-and-white 2D architectural CAD linework on pure white background

3) 인페인팅 · 아웃페인팅 / Pivot (Editing & Outpainting Control)
- 지정 영역만 수정: Edit only inside the specified zone; keep everything else unchanged
- Pivot 좌측 고정 확장: Keep the left-aligned original drawing completely locked and unchanged; extend only into the right white margin
- 선 두께·축척 유지: Match original line weight, drawing scale, and orthographic projection
- 이음새 자연 연결: Seamless continuity along the exterior boundary wall

4) 조경물 추가 (Landscape & Outdoor Elements)
- 우드 데크 테라스: Natural timber wood deck terrace with outdoor cafe seating and linear bench
- 다층 석재 플랜터: Low architectural concrete/stone planters along the terrace perimeter
- 다간형 수목·그라스: Multi-stem ornamental trees, native ornamental grasses, low evergreen shrubs
- 보행 포장·디딤석: Concrete pedestrian paving, rectangular stone stepping stones over gravel
- 점경 인물(선택): Subtle architectural scale figures walking naturally (no foreground crowding)

5) 환경 · 날씨 · 시간대 (Atmosphere, Weather & Context)
- 맑은 주간: Crisp clear daylight, soft natural architectural shadows
- 골든아워·석양: Warm late afternoon golden hour sunlight, long diagonal shadows, warm interior glow
- 흐린 확산광: Soft overcast diffused light, neutral architectural photography tone
- 비 온 뒤(젖은 포장): After-rain damp paving with subtle reflections, fresh foliage
- 계절·도시 맥락: Early autumn foliage, quiet neighborhood street context, ArchDaily photography aesthetic
